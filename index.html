Siri Paye Meadows – Nature's Hidden Gem in Pakistan

Siri Paye Meadows, located above Shogran in the Kaghan Valley, is a breathtaking highland known for its lush green pastures, cloud-covered hills, and stunning views of Makra Peak. The meadows remain a top favorite for nature lovers, trekkers, and photographers who want to escape into the serene beauty of the north.

Surrounded by dense pine forests and seasonal wildflowers, Siri Paye is accessible via jeep ride from Shogran and offers panoramic views and peaceful camping spots. Whether you're planning a hike or simply seeking a calm escape, Siri Paye promises a magical experience.
